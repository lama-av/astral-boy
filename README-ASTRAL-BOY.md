# Astral Boy

A desk AI companion with a virtual-pet personality, built on the open-source
[XiaoZhi ESP32](https://github.com/78/xiaozhi-esp32) firmware (MIT license).

Astral Boy talks with you by voice (XiaoZhi server), and lives on your desk as a
little character with needs, moods and reactions drawn on a 240x240 screen.

## Hardware

- ESP32-S3 N16R8 (16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM)
- ST7789 240x240 LCD
- INMP441 I2S microphone + MAX98357A I2S amplifier
- One BOOT button
- Board profile: `main/boards/bread-compact-wifi-lcd` (all Astral Boy code lives here)

## What Astral Boy adds to XiaoZhi

- **Virtual pet**: hunger, energy, mood and health that change over time (saved in flash,
  including time powered off). Mood works as "patience": rudeness makes him grumpy and he
  refuses to talk until you cuddle him. Neglect can make him sick or faint.
- **37 animated faces** in a flat style, generated by code, including sick, fainted, nauseous, dizzy, cold, rainy, focus and more.
- **Two one-button minigames**: "Hora do Lanche" (catch apples, the only way to eat) and
  "Caça-Estrelas" (catch stars, the sleep game), with records and background music.
- **Reactions to the environment**: startles at sudden loud sounds, headbangs to music,
  real weather faces (Open-Meteo), misses you after hours alone, meal-time reminders,
  "where is the internet?" when offline, idle animations (yawn, look around, whistle).
- **Synthesized sounds** on the device: boot jingle, dance song, game SFX mixed over music.
- **Pomodoro** with a focused face and celebrations.
- **Tools for the AI (MCP)**: pet status/mood events, games, dance, alarms, timer/stopwatch,
  internet radio, Wikipedia, weather, news, holidays, currency, moon phase.
- **Wi-Fi setup by phone** (BluFi, EspBlufi app) on branch `astral-boy-blufi`.

## Branches

- `astral-boy-blufi` — current version (Wi-Fi provisioning via BluFi)
- `astral-boy` — earlier stable version (Wi-Fi provisioning via hotspot)

## Building

ESP-IDF v6.1, target `esp32s3`, board `bread-compact-wifi-lcd`:

```
idf.py build
idf.py -p COMx flash
```

The face pack folder is set in `main/CMakeLists.txt` (`DEFAULT_EMOJI_COLLECTION`). The Astral Boy
face GIFs are not included in this repository yet; point it to your own folder or to `otto-gif`.
